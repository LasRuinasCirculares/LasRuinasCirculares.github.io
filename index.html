Jun Zhang 张俊

Affiliation Chinese Information Processing Center, Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences

I am currently pursuing a master's degree at the Chinese Information Processing Center, Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ISCAS).

In July 2025, I received my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from Soochow University. I was then admitted to graduate study through the recommendation-based admission pathway, without taking the national postgraduate entrance examination, and began my master's studies at ISCAS.

From September to December 2024, I was a natural language processing intern at Baidu, where I worked on data quality improvement and the replication of o1-style reasoning methods.

My research focuses on trustworthy and agentic large language models. I am interested in improving reasoning reliability, knowledge consistency, temporal understanding, tool-use capabilities, and the interpretability of long reasoning processes.

Research interests:

  • Trustworthy Large Language Models
  • Agentic LLMs and Tool Use
  • Reasoning Reliability, Knowledge Conflicts, and Temporal Understanding

Outside research: I enjoy running marathons, swimming, and playing badminton.
