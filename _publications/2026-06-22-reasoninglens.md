## Abstract

ReasoningLens transforms long, unstructured reasoning traces into interactive
hierarchies that separate high-level strategies from low-level execution. It
combines structured visualization with agentic auditing, automated error
detection, and tool-augmented verification to support the interpretation and
diagnosis of large reasoning models.
