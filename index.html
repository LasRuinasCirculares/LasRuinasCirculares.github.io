Jun Zhang

Chinese Information Processing Laboratory

I am a researcher at the Chinese Information Processing Laboratory. My research focuses on the reasoning abilities of large language models and multimodal agents.

I am particularly interested in temporal reasoning, knowledge conflicts, visual tool use, and the interpretation of long reasoning chains. My goal is to build intelligent systems that are more capable, transparent, and reliable.

Research interests:

  • Large Language Model Reasoning
  • Multimodal Agents and Visual Tool Use
  • Knowledge Conflicts and Temporal Reasoning
